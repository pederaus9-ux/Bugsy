## Notes for Claude (map builder)

- **Dropped on purpose:** Kwik Trip searches came back empty for Galesville, Independence, Strum, Ettrick, and Eleva (nearest KT to Galesville is Holmen, ~6 mi) — don't place stores that don't exist.
- **Water towers:** several are street-name matches (Tower Dr / Tower Rd) — the tower sits somewhere on that street; pin the exact tank against aerials.
- **Downtown strips marked approximate** are town-center points — the Main St street-level matches (Whitehall, Blair, Osseo, Galesville, Trempealeau village) are the reliable anchors.
- **Beaver Creek bridge** coords (44.07598, -91.36130) come from HistoricBridges.org's own map link for the listing; it's abandoned and sits just off US-53 south of Galesville where old Mill St crossed the creek.
- **Brady's Bluff** (44.0202, -91.4833): WI DNR, Wikipedia, and Mapcarta all agree within ~100 m.
- **Lock and Dam No. 6** (43.99972, -91.43833): Wikipedia/USACE; observation deck is on the Wisconsin side, a natural storm-watching set piece.
