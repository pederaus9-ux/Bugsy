- Horse cause (pure geometry, `penEditableLand()` vs the accepted layout): two horse paddock corners lie 2.39 m and 0.50 m beyond the 24 m farm capsule, covered only by the paddock's own accepted footprint. Any move leaves that footprint ("Keep the entire enclosure on editable farm land"), and the one move toward the capsule (east) puts fences on the stone path.
- Chicken cause (`pen-chicken-blockers-local-chromium141.log`): not a snapshot misclassification. The accepted yard encloses real scenery (tree at (-9,4), hay bales at (-6.4,-2.8), a bush and rocks at its edges); all six are baseline and correctly exempt from the interior rule. What refuses the moves is the barn 0.5 m east, the stone path west, a bush south, and moved fences, coop doorways and care routes colliding with that enclosed scenery.
- Only whole-pen `movePen` was probed. Reshaping (`moveVertex`, `moveEdge`, `moveStructure`, gates, runs) has not been measured for these two sites.
