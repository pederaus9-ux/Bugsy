# Bounded shared-movement contract checkpoint

Baseline `7017109e16c1d8b2aceef456f49b0c81fa7b0935`, unchanged A20 runtime. Acceptance was written before prototypes in `acceptance.md`. Everything added here is test-only; no protected runtime file has been edited, so no new runtime digest/amendment has yet been registered. The broad matrix remains paused. This is a partial geometry/design checkpoint, **not a verified gameplay fix**.

## What was proved

`tests/helpers/cow-rigid-sweep.mjs` implements one shared experimental predicate: complete root segment with the existing full 0.42 m padding and no containing-obstacle exemptions; convex body parts with vertical bounds; translation, yaw and linear local-shape change across the whole interval. It uses endpoint hulls inflated by a derived interpolation-error bound. For an angular subinterval theta, maximum local radius R and local vertex displacement D, the bound is `(R*theta^2 + 2*abs(theta)*D)/8`. This follows from bounding the second derivative of a linearly deforming point under linear yaw. Angular subdivision is capped at 64; invalid/unbounded requests fail closed. It is conservative for this **explicit interpolation model only**.

The original route predicate failed 8/10 new contract checks in `legacy-contract-red.log`. A test-driver error (VM lexical binding was not exported) is separately preserved in `legacy-red.log`, not counted as a behavioral RED. The rigid prototype then passed 10/10 (`rigid-candidate.log`). Two added animation-extension/height tests failed the rigid-only version (`morph-red.log`); the same predicate extended with the above deformation term passed all 12/12 (`morph-candidate.log`, 1.158 seconds total locally). No original project assertion was edited or relaxed.

The 12 checks cover retained A19/A20 entries, the old 0.05 m shrink, containing start/goal, thin-obstacle translation, collision occurring only mid-turn, clear turns and parallel travel, aligned narrow passage versus blocked sideways passage, actual overhang height, invalid inputs, deforming shape/height, and the unchanged rig's frozen-pose passage through all nine authored approach-to-dock segments plus turns in all five beds. Floor, meadow overlay and straw are identified support primitives, not silently removed solid posts; other recorded solids remain. These are prototype geometry checks, not live gait or full-herd qualification.

## Live-animation counterexample: do not integrate yet

The unchanged rig is not a linear interpolation of endpoint vertices. A coherent source-based probe places the normal breathing curve's maximum halfway through one 30 Hz interval. `cow3d.js:281` uses `body.scale.y = 1 + .007*sin(state.time*1.5+seed)`. The endpoints have scale `1.0069978126139298`, while the half-step has `1.007`. A real body-only skinned triangle (1679, vertex 5038) reaches y `1.4150186156460973`, exceeding the endpoint prism maximum `1.4150176550190383` by `9.606270590101218e-7` m. The unchanged root/yaw and both endpoint controls pass. This is a counterexample to the proposed enclosure, not a newly observed gameplay collision or a reason to change an existing tolerance.

`animation-enclosure-coherent-red.json` is the credited RED. The preceding `animation-enclosure-red.json` is retained but uncredited: setting the fixture clock followed by a zero-delta update left its initial pose stale, because the real rig returns on dt=0. The corrected fixture advances a real positive step and asserts the initial pose matches its clock; the counterexample persists. No production animation was altered. More endpoint samples alone would not establish a bound on all articulated motion.

## Narrow-corridor motion findings

The initial stationary-pose diagnostic (`corridor-geometry.json`) found no vertices in non-support solids at the 18 authored approach/dock positions. Zero included vertices is not proof of disjoint meshes. Its foundation hits include Float32 surface rounding and its straw hits are support classification, not claimed new post bugs.

A separate bounded actual-rig diagnostic (`corridor-motion.json`) walks an aligned seed-0 rig from each authored approach at the existing 0.8 m/s, then applies the existing dock-heading formula. It uses 30/60/120 Hz, with at most five simulated seconds per case and positive vertex/primitive inclusion checks. This is a new short feasibility probe, **not a substitute or changed deadline for the original 500-second full-herd acceptance**. All 27 cases completed in 27.08 seconds of local wall time; no command remains running.

- The five bed approaches/turns and robot approach have no included-vertex witness at each rate (18 cases). They are not certified collision-free by that absence.
- Both feed approaches and the drink approach have positive lower-leg witnesses at every rate (9 cases). At 30 Hz the feed witness occurs at frame 48, root `[-9.5,.08,-34.02000000000014]`, vertex 23927 `[-9.23225015085646,.24135391949114904,-33.32387680867184]`, strictly inside the real axis-aligned bunk base `[-10.8999996,.23999999,-33.375,-5.5,.35999998,-32.7249999]`.
- At 60/120 Hz the drink witness intersects the real trough side wall, not its water surface. Vertices 23921/23927 are weighted to `knee0` (~0.94) and `hoof0` (~0.06), so these are leg/solid contacts, not allowed mouth/food contact. The relevant primitives are axis-aligned boxes from `cow-yard.js:85–88`, so the cylinder/tilted-AABB limitation does not invalidate these positive witnesses.

Limitations: synthetic aligned initial posture, rig seed 0, no other animals or care-pose overlay, no full controller lifecycle or real browser rendering in this probe. This establishes that an always-forward approach with this valid gait phase is unsafe, not that the dock/corridor is fundamentally unusable. Slower physical approach, a safe gait-settling transition, or another executable approach remains to be investigated under unchanged speed ceilings and deadlines. No layout/rig redesign decision is justified yet.

## Targeted research and selected next implementation boundary

[Catto's continuous-collision material](https://box2d.org/files/ErinCatto_ContinuousCollision_GDC2013.pdf) covers shape distance and conservative advancement, including angular motion; rigid-body translation alone is insufficient. [Kavan and Zara's primary LBS collision paper](https://users.cs.utah.edu/~ladislav/kavan05fast/kavan05fast.pdf), sections 3–4, bounds deformed skin from its influencing transforms and refits hierarchy nodes only when needed. This offers a way to avoid the earlier per-vertex-observer cost. It does **not** supply temporal CCD for this game: its stated joint model excludes translation and scaling, and its convex-weight assumptions must account for this rig's stored Float32 weight sums without normalizing or changing them. The articulated Taylor-model paper was located, but full text was unavailable from the attempted publisher/archive sources; no uninspected algorithm is being presented as implemented proof.

Actionable boundary: retain the prototype as a geometry kernel, then supply conservative **animated** part bounds from the existing procedural rig and support/care overlays. Use a non-mutating pose/state preview or shadow rig so proposed translation, turning and settling can be checked before publishing actor motion or reservation progress. First prove exact snapshot/restore or shadow equivalence, then certify the temporal bounds against breathing, nonlinear IK/hoof release and care-pose transitions. A discrete bone-aware bound alone is not a temporal bound. Do not normalize skin weights, change gait, or patch the counterexample with an arbitrary epsilon.

Only after that prerequisite passes should a single acceptance predicate be wired into cow route edges, direct steps, sidesteps, rejoin and replanning, with the named protected-file amendment/digests recorded **before** edits. Keep all static obstacles, reservations, support/contact limits and separation; prove eventual physical arrival rather than permanent stopping. Then run the captured post cases and directly affected real browser care case before broad requalification. This is a technical evidence gap, not an outstanding owner-permission request, and it does not justify moving fixtures or changing rig geometry.

Current A20 care/wake credit remains 1/30; full physical safety remains RED. No runtime changes, remote uploads, Site updates, merge or deployment. Earlier historical passes/REDs and all original seeds/deadlines remain intact.
